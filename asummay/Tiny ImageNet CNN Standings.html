How to read the comparison

  • The fairest peers for your model are rows marked From scratch, 64px: trained only on the 100k Tiny ImageNet images at native size, one network, no teacher.
  • Distilled rows also train from scratch on Tiny ImageNet, but learn from a larger teacher network that was itself trained on Tiny ImageNet.
  • ImageNet-pretrained rows start from weights learned on 1.28M full-size ImageNet images (which contain Tiny ImageNet's classes) and usually upscale inputs to 224–299px. They are listed for scale, not as like-for-like competitors.
  • Rank (#) is recomputed by top-1 within whatever filters are active.

Caveats

  • Recipes differ widely: 10 to 1,200 epochs, different augmentation, different stems. A gap of 1–2 points between two papers is often recipe, not architecture.
  • Many sources, like your log, report the best epoch on the validation set they also report. Test-set numbers from the old Stanford leaderboard run 4–8 points lower than validation numbers.
  • Mixup-benchmark rows (OpenMixup, AdAutoMix, MixMo, Catch-up Mix) are tuned for 400–1,200 epochs; your run used 100.
  • Rows tagged ? have a weak source (an example log line, a blog post, an unclear split or an ambiguous model attribution).
  • No Tiny ImageNet results turned up for RepVGG, VanillaNet or ParNet, the modern plain-network designs.
  • A 30-class Tiny ImageNet subset study (arXiv 2505.08259) was excluded because its numbers are not comparable to the 200-class task.
